Before launch, confirm each source remains available under the current Unsplash License; verify the embedded crop has no legible private addresses, phone numbers, license plates, or other private identifiers; and confirm the use remains appropriate for the final product, jurisdictions, and brand. Replace any asset that does not pass that review.
